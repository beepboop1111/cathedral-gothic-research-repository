# Research sources for a new project

Extract the three ZIP files into the same folder. Upload the PDFs and any relevant notes to your new project. The ZIP files are transport packages; extract them first.

## Contents

- 01 Original Books: full PDFs available in Research, unchanged.
- 02 Plates and Illustrations: extracted historical plates, atlases, reference images, and geometry illustrations.
- 03 Research and Tools: registers, catalogues, research notes, translations, checkpoints, and V08/preprocessing tools. Scripts were copied without execution.

Byte-identical files are stored once. SOURCE_FILE_INDEX.csv maps every original file and archive member to its retained path and SHA-256. Different renderings and revisions are retained. Existing cathedral models and generators outside the research/source packages are excluded. No original files were modified.

## Coverage limits

This is an export of sources available in the selected Research folder, not a claim that every cited book is available in full. Selected-plate PDFs are not full editions. Historical sources do not establish locked cathedral geometry.
