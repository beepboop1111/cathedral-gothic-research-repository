## New sources supplied in the discussion

1. [Nate Bergin, *Evolution of the Church Floor Plan*, Part I](https://evolutionofarchitecture.com/2024/12/09/evolution-of-the-church-floor-plan/) (December 2024). Useful for basilica origins, longitudinal processional planning, transept and crossing modules.
2. [Part II](https://evolutionofarchitecture.com/2025/01/07/evolution-of-the-church-floor-plan-part-2/) (January 2025). Useful for centralized, square, octagonal, and ambulatory plan families; *not* a measured cathedral cloister comparison.
3. [Part III](https://evolutionofarchitecture.com/2025/03/27/evolution-of-the-church-floor-plan-part-3/) (March 2025). Useful for Renaissance and later variations, less directly applicable to our French Gothic precinct.
4. [European Architecture Tumblr — Comparative Plans of English Gothic Cathedrals](https://europeanarchitecture.tumblr.com/post/78850033030/comparative-plans-of-english-gothic-cathedrals-a). Identifies the historic comparative work of Banister Fletcher; use the **original** high-resolution plates rather than the reduced blog preview.
5. [Banister Fletcher, *Comparative Plans of English Cathedrals*, plate 410](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Comparative_Plans_of_English_Cathedrals_410.jpg), public-domain scan. Contains Ely, York, Winchester, Peterborough, Salisbury and Lincoln, plus a **0–300 ft** original scale.
6. [Banister Fletcher, plate 411](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Comparative_Plans_of_English_Cathedrals_411.jpg), listed as a companion plate; useful for Worcester, Canterbury, Gloucester, Norwich and Durham. Verify the original scan's scale and any figure-specific exceptions before quantitative tracing.

## Architectural implications for F5ZP

The precedents support investigating a **much closer church–cloister connection**, with the chapter house, refectory, and community circulation developed around the cloister perimeter. The F5ZP cloister's current short-side/church-length ratio (~0.286) is not inherently too large by comparison with Salisbury (~0.384) and Durham (~0.310, inferred). By contrast, the proposed 47-m library is a major mass beside the current ~70.66-m cathedral.

Before reworking rooms, compare **north and south attached-cloister arrangements on the unchanged cathedral**, then test alternatives to the library's uninterrupted 47 × 32-m envelope. Separate structural and buttress collision checks are necessary before approving any cathedral doorway.

**Lock discipline:** Neither a historic building's measured dimensions nor a blog figure supersedes the native F5ZP plan, the cathedral's 1:1 scale, or E5-C3-L1. Any rescaling or relocation requires an explicit new design decision and checkpoint.
