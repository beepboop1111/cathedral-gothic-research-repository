GOTHIC PLATE LIBRARY — INITIAL SOURCE SURVEY

This is a curated starting set, not an exhaustive verification of all uploaded books.
The register covers inspected source illustrations in two German volumes and a secondary list of research-located Viollet-le-Duc figures.
All original PDFs were left unchanged. Atlas reproduces whole original pages; no thresholding or cropping.
PDF page indexes are 1-based, independently of printed pages. Atlas pages mapped in original_atlas_page_map.csv.
Volume I is the uploaded 1901 edition; Volume II is the 1892 edition. Never mix third/fourth-edition figures.
Plate drawings are not automatically dimensionally authoritative. Rectify page skew, identify the drawn geometry, separate cutouts, then scale from explicit dimensions or user-provided control length.
Recommended tests: UM-II pages 281, 285, 291, 295, 299, 309, 313, 317.
For 2D extraction prefer arcs/circle-fit and SVG geometries before raster relief. Complex crossings require manual topology review.
