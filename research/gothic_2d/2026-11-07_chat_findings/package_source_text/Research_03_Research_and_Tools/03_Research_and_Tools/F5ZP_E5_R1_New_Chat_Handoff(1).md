2. Show how cathedral aisle access, cloister walk, chapter house, refectory, stairs, residential wings, garden entrance and discreet library passage connect **without forcing public traffic through the contemplative enclosure**.

4. Reassess **building footprint ratios** in context of the roughly 70.7-m current cathedral envelope: monastery and library must be substantial but subordinate to the cathedral. Consider articulated library mass rather than one 47-m rectangular block.
5. Provide **same-scale 2D plan drawings**, clear proposed/locked overlays, per-option measurements (actual boundaries, shortest wall/root gaps, site/sightline impacts) and a comparison table with evidence, assumptions, strengths, conflicts and open questions.
