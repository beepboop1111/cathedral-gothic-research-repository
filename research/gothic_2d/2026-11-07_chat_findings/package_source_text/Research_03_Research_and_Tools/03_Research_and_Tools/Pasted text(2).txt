## 5. Generator Compatibility

Evaluate every candidate against the following scripts.

| Script | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `sketch2stl.py` | Convert drawings into 3D reliefs and pierced Gothic tracery |
| `plan2stl.py` | Extract architectural forms from floor plans |
| `massing.py` | Generate simplified architectural volumes |
| `plan2geometry.py` | Recover measurable 2D vector geometry |
| `geometry2stl.py` | Convert extracted paths into reference STL geometry |
| `sweep2schem.py` | Convert moulding profiles into Minecraft schematic geometry |

Identify primary and secondary script compatibility.

Do not assume that a photograph, perspective, or shaded axonometric contains directly measurable geometry.

Distinguish between:

- Directly extractable geometric drawings.
- Drawings requiring reconstruction or interpretation.
- Visual architectural references.
- Historical or decorative references unsuitable for automated extraction.

| Field | Description |
|---|---|

| Drawing_Type | Plan, section, elevation, profile, construction, axonometric, photograph |

| Script_Secondary | Other compatible generators |
| Extraction_Quality | High, medium, low, or unassessed |

Do not invent missing page numbers, monument identifications, scales, or figure designations.

Use explicit unknown values.

Preserve page numbering distinctions between original printed books and digitized PDFs.

Where multiple illustrations appear on one page, create separate figure records when identifiable.

## 7. Quality and Priority Ranking

Evaluate candidates against:

1. Architectural relevance.
2. Image resolution and clarity.
3. Completeness of visible geometry.
4. Availability of construction lines.
5. Ability to reconstruct actual arcs and curves.
6. Presence of dimensions or scale.
7. Historical attribution and provenance.
8. Generator compatibility.
9. Amount of manual cleanup required.

Assign extraction priorities.

**Priority A:** Clean, directly extractable geometry.

**Priority B:** Useful geometry requiring reconstruction.

**Priority C:** Architectural reference requiring significant interpretation.

**Priority D:** Historical or visual reference only.

The priority ranking must not imply historical authenticity or dimensional accuracy.

## 10. Important Project Restrictions

This library supports the continuing Cathedral F5ZP architectural reconstruction project.

However:

- Do not change the approved cathedral master floor plan.
- Do not alter locked vault, rib, pier, or chevet geometry.
- Do not treat dimensions from historical books as approved cathedral dimensions.
- Do not generate STL files during this phase.
- Do not overwrite the original historical PDFs.
- Do not replace original drawings with AI-generated interpretations.

The purpose is to create a historically grounded architectural source library that can support future geometry-generation work.
