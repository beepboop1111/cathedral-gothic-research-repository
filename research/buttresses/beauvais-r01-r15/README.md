## Critical interpretation
- An inclined stone coping is not automatically a circular flying-arch extrados.
- Distinguish source-supported masonry edges from actual structural bearing contacts.
- Upper and lower flying-arch stages are separated by intermediate support; do not extrapolate across it.
- Burges plates 5/6, VLD figure 61 and Ungewitter 912 cannot be dimensionally overlaid as though one matched surveyed structure.
- R11/R12 pixel geometry cannot establish historic compass centers, actual stone joints or 1:1 dimensions.
- Burges labels MODERN do not identify the exact limits of repairs.
- F5ZL-C support schedule remains missing; structural capacity and approved F5ZP integration unresolved.
