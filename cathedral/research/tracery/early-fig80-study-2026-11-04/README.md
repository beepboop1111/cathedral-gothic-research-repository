## Historical interpretation and scope
The engraving shows a central circle with six surrounding lobes, two pointed lancets, an enclosing pointed arch, and masonry/glazing lines. The drawing does **not** independently supply an exact 3D section, actual masonry depth, physical dimensions, structural capacity, or proof of the intended medieval compass construction. The early R03 uses **one unioned six-lobed oculus cut**, rather than seven independent oculus apertures; it must not be confused with the *different later R03 three-layer/9-void study* in the main St. Leu register.
