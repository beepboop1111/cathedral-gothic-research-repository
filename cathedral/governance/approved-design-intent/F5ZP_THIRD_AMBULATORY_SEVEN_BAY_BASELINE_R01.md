## Locked organizational principles

2. Seven principal chapel entrances, one per bay. The **90° axial** entrance is uninterrupted and monumentally dominant; the 70°/110° companion entrances are broad; other chapel entrances step down in prominence.
3. One common corner support at each adjacent outer-bay boundary, **not doubled piers**. Vault divisions are not ground-level walls obstructing circulation.
4. Continuous, generously open sideways circulation through all seven third-ambulatory bays, and open connections inward to the protected middle ambulatory.
5. **Seven principal connections inward**, corresponding to established middle-ambulatory bay rhythm and protected bearing positions; not fourteen separate passage openings.
6. **30° and 150° shoulder bays** are adaptable to straight choir aisle junctions while keeping the sector rhythm where possible; protected choir geometry must not be moved.
