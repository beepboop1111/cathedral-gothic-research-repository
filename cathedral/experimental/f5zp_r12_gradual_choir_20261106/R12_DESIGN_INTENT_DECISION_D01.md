# F5ZP R12 — Design Intent Decision D01
Date: 2026-11-06

## Accepted architectural direction
Adopt a gradual visual approach from the nave through the straight choir into the chevet, building anticipation without abrupt or arbitrary separator changes. The choir should read as distinct, with increasing architectural emphasis toward the east. Preserve the curved chevet as a distinct radial architectural system.

## Reference geometry status
- R11 remains the current versioned **experimental 2D floor-plan reference**, not promoted to approved or locked geometry.
- R12 remains an **experimental visual comparison**, not a masonry or support approval.
- Nave regular arcade division: 200 native units; choir Q2–Q5 arcade interbay divisions: 270, exact shared axes with 520 main separators; transverse schedule 320/197/292.
- Q1–Q2: existing 470 aisle masonry reservation and 1160 main transition; wider threshold stays in place provisionally.
- The 235-unit central emphasis in R12 is a **visual proposal only** inside the 470-unit reservation. No physical reduction, opening, support width or structural feasibility is approved.
- Q1–Q2 aisle separator remains offset 25 west of its main separator axis. No hidden coordinate changes approved.
- Chevet stays on its existing protected seven-bay radial geometry; do not impose a flat-bay 270 division.
- Existing vault dimensions, crossing and protected geometry unchanged.

## Next architectural test
Investigate actual Q1–Q2 bearing geometry and potential arcade openings, including the role and visibility of the proposed 235 visual center. Compare alternatives against protected vault clear spaces and existing R11 support constraints. Validate geometry separately from historical / architectural / structural adequacy. Do not create openings or alter support geometry without review.

## Governance
Design direction **accepted**. Geometry **experimental**, not approved or locked. No generator, dimensions, or historical originals replaced. Missing F5ZL-C support schedule remains unresolved.
