## Key findings

1. The historical drawing supports measurable **candidate circular construction**, not proof of exact compass centers/radii or a physical 1:1 engraving scale.
2. The modular generator can produce configurable buttress assemblies; masonry and drainage are incomplete architectural prototypes.
3. The R06 experimental seats have positive measured volume overlaps; this is NOT masonry bond or structural capacity validation.
4. The R07 saved 90° experimental bay contains 16 unchanged R21-B reference objects and 60 watertight new meshes, producing positive 8/8 contact checks and zero BVH surface crossings against 12 selected vault/rib/spring meshes.
5. R21-B, trial pier cores and outer pads are NOT approved structural supports. F5ZL-C support data remain missing. No dimensions or support capacity have been invented.

## Integration route toward cathedral generator

1. Recover approved 1:1 receiving-wall faces and vertical bearing datums.
2. Resolve F5ZL-C support schedule or keep explicit uncertainty and experimental proxy geometry.

4. Add historically sourced masonry sections, voussoir bonding, water-management and proportion options **after** interface geometry is established.
5. Promote no trial geometry without the required historical, architectural and structural checks plus user approval.
