# F5ZP buttress historical evidence — R01 inventory / R02 comparison

**Status: HISTORICAL RESEARCH + EXPERIMENTAL COMPARISON. NOT APPROVED ARCHITECTURE.**

This checkpoint publishes the recovered buttress-plate catalog and the R02 historical comparison for the three-chapel chevet junction. The complete ZIP preserves nine plate excerpts, original inventory material, measured/review records, station SVG, comparative scripts, and notes. The `browsable/` directory exposes findings without requiring ZIP extraction.

## Principal findings

- R01 plate research indexed **81 images**: 34 flying buttresses, 27 buttress elevations, 15 plans/sections and five statics diagrams; source files remain unaltered.
- Historical patterns studied include integrated wall receivers, T-shaped receiving plans and intermediate piers, notably from Ungewitter and Viollet-le-Duc.
- Saved R21 comparison retains **A = 70°/90°/110°** and **B = 50°/90°/130°**. B is **recommended for further review**, not approved.
- R21 counted **9** cross-chapel wall/cheek surface-intersection pairs for A and **0** for B in the specific tested subset. These are not whole-building collision certificates.
- The missing **F5ZL-C** physical-support schedule prevents confirmation of actual approved receiving faces, eccentricities, or capacity.
- The springing reference difference (**Z2300 trial outer vs Z3000 protected inner**) remains unresolved; gallery, chapel passages, windows and roof/drainage coordination are open.
- Neither the historical precedent nor surface contact proves structural adequacy. M01 remains open.
