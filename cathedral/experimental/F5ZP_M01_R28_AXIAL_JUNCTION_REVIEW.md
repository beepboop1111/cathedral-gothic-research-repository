# R28 — axial junction recovery and measured review

EXPERIMENTAL. M01 OPEN. No architectural promotion. 2026-11-05.

This package isolates exact saved geometry from R27 arrangement B and the persistently corrected R22 axial V13 comparator. It is a diagnostic model, not a corrected vault generator. No source geometry was changed or regenerated. Only review materials and camera settings differ in the copy.

## Actual findings
- Two axial third-ambulatory half-webs: zero nonmanifold edges; minimum triangle area 799.1328112397838 native units squared.
- 89 samples of the saved inward arch against the provisional V13 surface: maximum distance 67.01972961425781 native units, minimum 0.000732421875.
- Each half-web has 14 triangle-pair intersections with the provisional comparator. These are surface diagnostics, not solid-volume penetration measurements or proof either source is wrong.
- The protected inner spring Z3000 and trial outer spring Z2300 retain their 700-unit difference.
- Independent native reopen matched all recorded geometry signatures. GLB export/import: 59 mesh objects, maximum world-bound discrepancy zero, tolerance 0.02 native unit.
- Source-file hashes matched. F5ZN-A, working generators, Pass 6J and historical sources were untouched. Discarded outer-ring geometry was not restored.

## Reproduction
build_r28.py requires the pinned MR COMPUTER source paths documented in the script and Blender 5.0.1. It reads source files only and creates a separate review directory. verify_r28.py reopens the generated native scene and round-trips the GLB; source hashes and mesh signatures are in R28_QA.json. make_sections.py uses Matplotlib and the packaged sections JSON without Blender. The original generator is not executed.

## Genuine blockers to proper full generation
The actual approved inner/middle generator and web-interface authority have not been identified; the recovered V13 registration remains provisional. The approved F5ZL-C receiving-face/support schedule is missing. Arbitrarily warping the new webs to this comparator would not establish architectural correctness. The fourteen-vault support topology, supported spring-level transition, vestibule roof junction, complete openings, whole-cathedral fit, historical construction and structural capacity remain open. Arrangement B is a study candidate, not an approved station choice.

Next work must establish the authoritative middle-web boundary and receiving masonry. New receiving solids can be studied experimentally, but cannot stand in for recovered approved supports. No architectural approval is requested for this diagnostic package.
